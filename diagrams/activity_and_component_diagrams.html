🎯 Clinical Lifecycle Flow

  • Initial Node Black filled circle initiating donor check-in.
  • Decision 1 Age check guard: [18 <= age <= 65] vs [age < 18 or > 65] reject.
  • Decision 2 90-day cooldown guard: [days >= 90 or New] vs [days < 90] active block.
  • Decision 3 Hemoglobin check: [Hgb >= 12.0 g/dL] vs [< 12.0 g/dL] deferral.
  • Phlebotomy Successful collection of 1 Unit (450 mL whole blood).

⚡ Concurrency & Synchronization

  • Fork Bar Solid horizontal synchronization bar dividing control flow into concurrent parallel threads.
  • Thread 1: Donor Care Refreshments, recovery post-care rest, followed by certificate issuance & SMS notification.
  • Thread 2: Stock Update Atomic database increment in blood_inventory, followed by buffer safety re-evaluation.
  • Join Bar Synchronization bar awaiting completion of both parallel branches before session finalization.
  • Final Node Standard UML bullseye terminating session logging.

📦 Structural Notations (UML 2.5)

  • Component Classifier Standard rectangular box carrying <<component>> stereotype with top-right 2-tab glyph.
  • Boundary Ports Small square boxes on outer component perimeter defining explicit interaction gates.
  • Provided Interfaces (Lollipops) Circles -o declaring capabilities offered by the system: IDonorService, IEligibilityCheck, IHospitalRequisition.
  • Required Interfaces (Sockets) Semicircles -() declaring external dependencies: ISMSGateway, IGovtHealthRegistry.
  • Assembly Connectors Ball-and-socket joints -o)- binding internal modules with zero tight coupling.

🧩 Sub-Component Responsibilities

  • Eligibility & Screening Engine Central coordinator validating age [18-65], weight >=45kg, 90-day cooldown, vitals, and hemoglobin.
  • DonorManagement Manages donor records, demographic profiles, contact addresses, and donation logs.
  • HospitalRequisition Handles emergency orders, compatibility algorithms, and hospital priority matching.
  • BloodInventory Maintains unit balances across all 8 ABO/Rh types and enforces the 10-unit buffer threshold.
  • DonorAnalytics Geospatial density tracking, predictive shortage alerting, and supply heatmaps.
